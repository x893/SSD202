2. Burn the kernel in uboot

fatload  mmc 0:1  0x21000000 uImage.xz
nand erase.part KERNEL
nand write.e 0x21000000 KERNEL ${filesize}
nand erase.part RECOVERY
nand write.e 0x21000000 RECOVERY ${filesize}
